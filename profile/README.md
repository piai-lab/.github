We build AI-ready scientific infrastructure and study agents that carry inquiry from hypothesis to evidence. Our work is meant for every field of science, and biomedicine is where we begin.

## Research

### AI-ready scientific infrastructure

We connect data, models, tools and knowledge so that agents can find, use and verify them, giving scientific reasoning, computation and validation common ground

### Autonomous scientific discovery

We study how agents can choose experiments, test predictions against evidence and revise their explanations, closing the loop of inquiry

构建 AI-ready 科学基础设施，研究智能体如何从假设走向证据。πAI Lab 面向各个科学领域，以生物医学为切入点。

### 研究方向

**AI-ready 科学基础设施**

贯通数据、模型、工具与知识，让智能体能够查找、调用和核验，为科学推理、计算与验证提供共同基础

**自主科学发现**

研究智能体如何自主选择实验、用证据检验预测、修正解释，让科学探究形成闭环
