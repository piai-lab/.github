## πAI Lab

**Towards autonomous science**

We build AI-ready scientific infrastructure and study agents that carry inquiry from hypothesis to evidence. Our work is meant for every field of science, and biomedicine is where we begin.

[Website](https://piai-lab.github.io/) · [liuzaoqu@163.com](mailto:liuzaoqu@163.com)

### Research

- **AI-ready scientific infrastructure**: connecting data, models, tools and knowledge so that agents can find, use and verify them
- **Autonomous scientific discovery**: agents that choose experiments, test predictions against evidence and revise their explanations
