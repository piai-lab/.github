# πAI Lab

**Open intelligence infrastructure for scientific discovery.**

πAI Lab is a public research and open-technology organization initiated by the πHub big-science program in Guangzhou. We start from real biomedical research and develop scientific infrastructure that can remain useful across models, agents, projects, and disciplines.

Our goal is not to build another closed, all-in-one scientific agent. We work on the foundations that let researchers and agents access data correctly, invoke methods under explicit conditions, inspect claims against source evidence, and sustain long-running research. Scientists remain responsible for research direction, interpretation, and conclusions.

## Research system under development

The following describes intended capability boundaries and active research directions. It does not represent a set of completed or publicly released systems.

### I. Scientific foundations

- **OmniData** — research on agent-native access to scientific data with semantics, versions, quality, permissions, updates, and provenance intact.
- **OmniEngine** — research on organizing scientific methods as reusable capabilities with applicability conditions, reproducible environments, validation, and explicit failure returns.
- **OmniScholar / OmniPatent** — research on evidence-grounded knowledge for scholarly literature and patent workflows, preserving document structure, citations, figures, and source relationships. OmniPatent is in development.
- **OmniHarness** — research on governed execution for long-running work: task state, tools, permissions, observability, verification, recovery, and handoff.

### II. Research progression

- **OmniMind** — a research workbench under development for maintaining questions, hypotheses, evidence, and task state while coordinating analysis and tool use.

### III. Question formation

- **OmniSage** — research on how worthwhile, testable scientific questions might be formed, differentiated by evidence, and evaluated without replacing scientific judgment.

These are not parallel products at the same maturity level. The three layers address different problems: making scientific resources usable, keeping a research process moving, and forming questions worth investigating.

## Applied research directions under development

- **Scientific communication** — scientific plotting, editable scientific illustration, presentations, and document workflows through OmniPlotter, OmniSketch, OmniSlide, and OmniOffice.
- **Drug discovery** — research on domain data, methods, and agent workflows designed to share the same scientific foundations rather than form a separate demonstration stack.
- **Evaluation and reproducibility** — benchmarks, provenance, failure analysis, recovery, and validation in real research settings.

Directions listed here may be at different stages of research and development. Inclusion does not imply a public release, production readiness, or scientific, clinical, legal, or regulatory validation.

## How we work

- **Evidence before confidence.** Claims remain traceable to data, sources, or explicit assumptions.
- **Reproducibility by design.** Inputs, environments, methods, outputs, and limitations should be inspectable.
- **Human scientific accountability.** AI can extend research work; people remain responsible for decisions and releases.
- **Open interfaces.** Foundational capabilities should be portable and useful beyond a single model or application.
- **Built from real science.** We begin in biomedicine, where domain constraints, data quality, and validation can be examined rigorously, then generalize what survives contact with practice.
- **Engineering, product, and research together.** Engineering creates reliable capabilities, products bring them into use, and research turns real problems into transferable methods and knowledge.

## 中文摘要

**πAI Lab · 面向科学发现的开放智能基础设施**

πAI Lab 由广东智慧医学国际研究院 πHub 大科学计划发起。我们从真实生物医学研究出发，建设能够跨模型、跨 Agent、跨课题持续复用的科学基础设施，而不是再做一个封闭的端到端科研智能体。

我们的研究体系分为三层：OmniData、OmniEngine、OmniScholar 与 OmniHarness 构成数据、方法、证据知识和可靠执行地基；OmniMind 组织已有问题的持续研究；OmniSage 研究值得解决的问题如何形成、区分与验证。OmniPatent 与 OmniScholar 同属证据知识方向，面向专利工作流，目前处于建设中。

科学家始终保留研究方向、证据判断和最终结论责任。当前公开页面表达组织使命、研究体系和建设方向；方向被列入不代表已经公开发布、达到生产状态，或获得科学、临床、法律与监管认可。
